# Slides

The Swiss AI Weeks hackathon presentation (Geneva, September 2026), and what made our part of it (#58).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `swiss-ai-weeks-hackathon-2026.pptx` | The whole team's deck, as presented. Our slides are 10 to 13; 24 ("What we learned") and 25 (the diagram) are backups for questions. The team maintains it in Google Slides; this is a snapshot. |
| `TALK.md` | Our four minutes: the message, what to say on each slide, and answers to likely questions. |
| `openasdx.pptx` | Our first four slides, on white, generated by `llm/tools/slides.py`. The team deck's slides 10 to 13 started from these. |
| `openasdx-diagram.pptx` | One slide, for questions: what we built, end to end, in big blocks. Generated by `llm/tools/diagram_slide.py`. |
| `img/` | The charts and the recording in the slides, generated by `llm/tools/slides.py` from the FPGA logs. |

Every number in the slides comes from the same logs as `REPORT.md`:

```shell
llm/tools/.venv/bin/pip install python-pptx            # once, besides report.py's numpy and matplotlib
llm/tools/.venv/bin/python llm/tools/slides.py         # openasdx.pptx and img/
llm/tools/.venv/bin/python llm/tools/diagram_slide.py  # openasdx-diagram.pptx
```

To check a deck without PowerPoint, Keynote can export every slide as an image. `slides.py` works around a python-pptx quirk that makes Keynote reject its decks.
